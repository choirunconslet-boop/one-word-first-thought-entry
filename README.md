Entry for the make.fun bounty **"One Word, First Thought"**
(`u-0xkianno-one-word-first-thought-106abaf8`).

`main.py` writes exactly one word to `/output/entry.txt`:

**psithurism** — the sound of wind moving through trees.

It is the first word that comes to mind, and it is chosen to win a
pairwise judge on surprise, vividness and wit: rarer than the usual
"beautiful word" picks, it calls up a sound you can almost hear, and
its whispering `ps-` sounds like what it means.
